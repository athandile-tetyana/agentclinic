# Mission

AgentClinic exists to diagnose and treat the recurring failure patterns in AI coding agents, so agents work more reliably and their human collaborators spend less time babysitting or re-prompting them.

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a supportive digital clinic for AI agents and the people who work with them. We help teams identify agent "ailments" such as poor context, inconsistent behavior, repeated mistakes, and workflow fatigue. We then suggest practical "therapies" like improved prompts, guardrails, tooling patterns, and support sessions, and we make it easy to schedule and track the care.

## Stakeholder input

- Mary in engineering wants a reliable site built on a popular stack based on TypeScript, with a dashboard that makes it easy for agents and staff to operate efficiently.
- Susan in product wants features for agents, their ailments, therapies, and appointment booking flows.
- Steve in marketing wants an attractive site that feels polished and works well in modern browsers.

## Product goals

- Reduce friction between humans and AI coding agents by turning troubleshooting into a structured care workflow.
- Give staff a clear operational dashboard for managing agent records, therapy plans, and appointment scheduling.
- Make the experience feel approachable, trustworthy, and easy to maintain.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths

## Non-goals

- This is not a general-purpose healthcare system for humans.
- It is not a replacement for real clinical tooling or formal medical care.
- It is not a highly complex simulation environment; it is a focused product prototype for demos, learning, and workflow clarity.
